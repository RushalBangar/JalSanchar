THE CHALLENGE • SYSTEM BREAKDOWNS

Why Traditional Rural Water Supply Fails

During seasonal droughts, millions of liters of public drinking water are diverted, delayed, or unverified. JalSanchar eliminates three systemic vulnerabilities:

22% no_transfer

Estimated Water Diversion

Without cryptographic verification, water tankers frequently divert public emergency water to unauthorized industrial buyers, leaving remote village tanks bone dry.

JalSanchar Fix: Strict 500m geofencing triggers auto-lock on driver escrow payments if route diverges.
Zero warning

Advance Shortage Warning

Traditional administration only reacts after village wells run dry. Citizens are forced to wait days for emergency tenders, bureaucratic tenders, and logistical approval.

JalSanchar Fix: NASA SMAP & Sentinel-2 telemetry forecasts well exhaustion 14 days ahead.
Reactive assignment_late

Manual Grievance Redressal

Panchayat leaders spend hours submitting physical complaints to Taluka offices. Lack of transparent digital audit trails delays emergency tanker authorization.

JalSanchar Fix: Automated mobile attestation triggers next tanker dispatch automatically.
THREE-TIER GOVERNANCE

How JalSanchar Solves the Water Crisis

A reliable 3-stage process combining satellite intelligence, GPS tracking, and QR verification.

1
STAGE 01 • PREDICT

1. Early Shortage Warning

Using weather radar and NASA SMAP satellite data, we predict which village wells will run dry up to 14 days in advance, so water tankers are sent before taps run completely dry.

2
STAGE 02 • PREVENT

2. Stop Water Theft & Diversion

Tanker drivers must scan a time-bound QR code at the village water tank with local Gram Panchayat officials. If a tanker deviates 500m off-route, payment is frozen automatically.

3
STAGE 03 • SUSTAIN

3. Fair Daily Water For Everyone

Smart route optimization guarantees at least 40 liters daily for every person and 70 liters for cattle, ensuring even the most remote hamlets receive timely deliveries.
