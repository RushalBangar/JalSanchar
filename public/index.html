THE CHALLENGE • SYSTEM BREAKDOWNS

Why Traditional Rural Water Supply Fails

During seasonal droughts, millions of liters of public drinking water are diverted, delayed, or unverified. JalSanchar eliminates three systemic vulnerabilities:

22% no_transfer

Estimated Water Diversion

Without cryptographic verification, water tankers frequently divert public emergency water to unauthorized buyers, leaving village tanks empty.

JalSanchar Fix: Strict 500m geofencing triggers auto-lock on driver escrow payments if route diverges.
Zero warning

Advance Shortage Warning

Traditional administration only reacts after wells run dry. Citizens wait days for tenders and logistical clearances.

JalSanchar Fix: NASA SMAP & Sentinel-2 telemetry forecasts well exhaustion 14 days ahead.
Reactive assignment_late

Manual Grievance Redressal

Panchayat leaders spend hours submitting physical complaints. Lack of digital audit trails delays tanker authorization.

JalSanchar Fix: Automated mobile attestation triggers next tanker dispatch automatically.
